# Vrinda-Store-Annual-Analysis-Using-Excel


## 📘 Project Overview
The **Vrinda Store Annual Sales Report 2022** aims to analyze the company’s online sales data to identify customer trends, channel performance, and sales opportunities.  
This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to clean, analyze, and visualize sales insights through an **interactive dashboard** that supports data-driven decision-making for 2023.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To create an interactive dashboard to analyze the online sales data of a clothing company  
to track the key metrics and draw insights that will help in decision-making.

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning Steps
Before analysis, raw data was cleaned and standardized in Excel:
1. **Removed Duplicates:** Data → Remove Duplicates  
2. **Standardized Text Values:**  
   - Gender → “Men” / “Women”     
3. **Added Derived Columns:**  
   - `Age Group` → Teenager / Adult / Senior  
   - `Month` → Extracted from Date  
4. **Handled Missing Data** using filters and replacements where required.

---

## 📈 Dashboard KPIs and Analysis

| No. | Business Question | Insight / Purpose |
|-----|-------------------|------------------|
| 1️⃣ | Compare **Sales vs Orders (Month-wise)** | To analyze performance trends across months |
| 2️⃣ | Show **Total Orders by Month** | Identify high-performing sales months |
| 3️⃣ | Which **Gender** contributes more to sales? | Understand gender-based buying patterns |
| 4️⃣ | Which **Age Group** drives the most revenue? | Focus marketing on profitable demographics |
| 5️⃣ | Which **Product Category** performs best? | Identify top-selling items |
| 7️⃣ | Which **Channel** performs best? | Find the top online platform |
| 8️⃣ | Which **State/City** contributes most? | Optimize regional logistics and marketing |

## 📊 Key Insights from Dashboard

### 💰 Financial Overview
- **Highest Sales Month:** March 2022  
- **Lowest Sales Period:** Oct–Dec 2022  
- **Strong Correlation** between total orders and total revenue

### 👩‍💼 Customer Demographics
- Women contributed **64%** of total sales  
- **Adult Women (30–50 years)** are the largest customer group (34.6%)  
- Teenage women also form a strong secondary audience (21.1%)

### 🌍 Geographic Insights
- **Top 5 States:** Maharashtra (₹2.99M), Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu  
- Focus regions for marketing and inventory expansion

### 🛒 Channel Performance
- **Amazon** dominates with **35% of total orders**  
- **Flipkart** (22%) and **Myntra** (23%) follow closely  
- E-commerce marketplaces are the major revenue sources

### 📦 Order Fulfillment
- **Delivered Orders:** 92%  
- **Returned/Refunded/Cancelled:** Only 8% combined  
- Indicates **good customer satisfaction and strong product quality**

---

## ✅ Recommendations for 2023

- Focus marketing campaigns on **Adult and Teenage Women**  
- Strengthen performance on **Myntra and Flipkart** while maintaining **Amazon**  
- Expand logistics in **top-performing states**  
- Launch **festive or discount campaigns** in *October–December* to improve sales dip
